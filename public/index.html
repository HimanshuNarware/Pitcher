Do NOT use your personal Gmail password. Use a Google App Password.

How to generate a Google App Password (1 min)
  1. Go to Google Account Security.
  2. Ensure 2-Step Verification is enabled.
  3. Go to App Passwords.
  4. Create an app named "Auto Cold Sender" and click Create.
  5. Copy the 16-character code (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop) and paste it above!
