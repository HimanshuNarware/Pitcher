Auto Cold Email Sender Flowchart

Detailed lifecycle depicting credential setup, zero-disk in-memory spreadsheet ingestion, live SSE execution, and automatic data wiping.

Template & Anti-Spam Setup"] K --> L["Pre-filled Cold Email Template Loaded
